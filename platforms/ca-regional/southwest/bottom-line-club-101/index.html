First Meeting? What to Expect
  • The only requirement is a desire to stop using cocaine and all other mind-altering substances.
  • There are no dues or fees. A basket may go around; you never have to put anything in it.
  • You do not have to speak. You can just listen. You can say "I'll pass."
  • You do not need to have anything figured out. Arrive a little early if you'd like to meet people.
  • On Zoom, you can keep your camera off and use just a first name.
  • When you are ready, you can make Bottom Line your home group. Ask anyone after the meeting how that works.
